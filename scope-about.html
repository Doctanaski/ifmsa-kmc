01 — What We Do

Clinical exchanges worldwide

SCOPE is IFMSA's largest standing committee, running professional exchange programs that give medical students hands-on clinical experience in hospitals around the world.

Outbound Exchanges

Send KMC students to clinical rotations at partner hospitals abroad — gaining exposure to different healthcare systems, specialties, and patient populations across continents.

Inbound Hosting

Welcome international medical students to Peshawar, pairing them with local hospitals and supervisors for meaningful clinical attachments in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

Pre-Departure Training

Prepare students for life and work abroad with workshops on cultural adaptation, clinical expectations, visa processes, and safety — ensuring every exchange is a confident one.

Alumni Network

Stay connected with exchange alumni across cohorts — a growing network of KMC graduates who've trained internationally and carry that experience into their careers.

02 — Our Focus Areas

Where exchanges make a difference

Every exchange is built around four pillars that shape how students learn, grow, and connect through professional exchange programs.

01

Clinical Rotations

Hands-on placements in hospitals across five continents — surgery, internal medicine, paediatrics, and more — giving students real clinical responsibility under supervision.

02

Cross-Cultural Learning

Living and working in a different country teaches adaptability, empathy, and communication — skills that make you a better physician, no matter where you practise.

03

Professional Networking

Build connections with fellow exchange students, host-country clinicians, and IFMSA members worldwide — a professional network that lasts well beyond the exchange.

04

Global Health Exposure

See how healthcare systems differ around the world — from resource-limited settings to advanced tertiary centres — and develop a truly global perspective on medicine.
