Public Health.

From community health screenings in Peshawar to campus vaccination drives and school health literacy outreach — SCOPH turns medical knowledge into visible public health impact.

What we do ↓

01 — What We Do

Public health in action

We bring health services directly to the people who need them — on campus, in schools, and across Peshawar's communities.

Community Screenings

Monthly health camps in Peshawar where we offer blood pressure checks, blood sugar testing, BMI assessments, and basic health consultations — free of charge.

Vaccination Drives

Campus pop-up immunisation sessions in collaboration with provincial health authorities — making vaccines accessible to students and the surrounding community.

School Outreach

Health literacy sessions in local schools — teaching children and adolescents about hygiene, nutrition, mental health, and preventive care through interactive workshops.

Environmental Audits

Campus health and climate assessments that evaluate air quality, waste management, water safety, and green space — producing actionable reports for institutional change.

02 — Our Focus Areas

Where we direct our energy

Four pillars guide every project, event, and campaign that SCOPH runs on campus and beyond.

01

Community Health

Direct health services for underserved populations — screenings, consultations, and referrals that bridge the gap between medical students and the communities around them.

02

Prevention & Immunity

Vaccination advocacy, disease prevention campaigns, and immunisation awareness — building herd immunity one campus event at a time.

03

Health Literacy

Empowering people with the knowledge to make informed health decisions — from school workshops to social media campaigns that debunk myths and spread evidence-based information.

04

Environmental Health

Auditing the environments we live and study in — assessing air quality, water safety, waste systems, and climate risks to push for healthier campuses and cities.
