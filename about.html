From a handful of medical schools in 1951 to a global federation of 1.5 million students in over 130 countries — this is the story of IFMSA, how our chapter plugs into it, and what we're here to do.

Our history ↓

01 — History

The federation behind the chapter

IFMSA began in 1951 in Copenhagen, when seven medical students from five European countries gathered to share ideas. What started as a conversation became the world's largest student-run organisation.

01

1951

Founded in Copenhagen

Seven students from five countries meet at the first international medical students' congress. The International Federation of Medical Students' Associations is born — a platform built by students, for students.

02

1968

Official status with the WHO

IFMSA gains official relations with the World Health Organization, giving medical students a formal seat in global health policy. Our voice now reaches the rooms where health decisions are made.

03

Today

A movement of 1.5 million

Over 130 National Member Organisations across six continents. Six standing committees, dozens of projects, and thousands of exchanges — all run by students, all serving the communities around us.

02 — Structure

How local chapters work

The federation runs top-down and bottom-up at once. National organisations represent countries, local chapters like ours bring the work to campus, and every member shapes both.

IFMSA International

The global federation sets vision, policy, and standards. It represents medical students to the WHO and UN, and coordinates international projects and exchanges across all six regions.

National Member Organisation

In Pakistan, IFMSA-Pakistan is the sole national organisation. It connects our chapter to the global body, relays policy and opportunities, and supports every local chapter nationwide.

Local Chapters

KMC is one of many local chapters. Local Committee Officers (LCOs) lead the six standing committees on campus, run events, and make membership real for every student here.

You

Membership is open to every KMC student. You pick a committee, join a project, attend an exchange — and in doing so you become part of the world's largest student network in medicine.

03 — Objectives

What we exist to do

Six standing committees, one shared mission. Each objective below maps to the work our committees run on campus and through IFMSA-Pakistan.

01

Advance medical education

We shape how medicine is taught — peer teaching, curriculum input, and educational exchanges that expose students to different systems and approaches across the world.

02

Create clinical & research opportunity

Through international exchanges and research projects, we give students hands-on clinical and scientific experience long before graduation.

03

Improve public health

We run awareness campaigns, screenings, and community projects on campus and beyond — turning medical knowledge into visible community impact.

04

Advocate for rights in health

From reproductive rights to the health of refugees, we advocate for health as a human right — in policy, on campus, and in public discourse.

05

Build the next generation of leaders

Every project, exchange, and workshop is leadership training in disguise. We develop students who think globally and act locally — in medicine and beyond.

04 — What we aim to do

A generation that treats medicine as a global act.

Our aim is simple: make sure every KMC student who wants to can step into the world stage of medicine — confident, connected, and equipped. No prior experience needed. Just the desire to be part of something bigger.

  • Make global health opportunities visible and accessible to every member
  • Run a chapter where students lead, not just attend
  • Connect KMC to a network of 1.5 million students worldwide
