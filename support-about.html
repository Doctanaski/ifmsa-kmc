Public Health in Practice

The Support Division powers PubSD — our student-driven publication platform for research abstracts, case reports, and public health commentary. We give KMC students a voice in the global health conversation.

Learn more ↓

01 — What We Do

Empowering student voices in public health

The Support Division provides the infrastructure, mentorship, and platform for medical students to publish their research, share clinical insights, and contribute to public health discourse.

Research Publications

Publish research abstracts, original studies, and systematic reviews authored by KMC students — giving their work visibility in a structured, peer-reviewed format.

Case Reports

Document and share unusual or instructive clinical cases encountered during rotations — building a living library of clinical learning for the entire student body.

Public Health Commentary

Offer timely commentary on health policy, community health issues, and global health trends — training students to think critically about the systems they will serve.

Mentorship & Training

Connect students with faculty and senior peers for guidance on scientific writing, referencing, and academic publishing — skills that extend far beyond the publication itself.

02 — Our Focus Areas

Where support makes a difference

Every publication is built around four pillars that shape how students learn, write, and contribute through the Support Division.

01

Academic Writing

Develop scientific writing skills through hands-on publication — from structuring abstracts to formatting references, building a foundation for lifelong academic contribution.

02

Evidence-Based Practice

Bridge the gap between clinical experience and published evidence — encouraging students to question, investigate, and contribute to the body of medical knowledge.

03

Health Awareness

Translate complex public health topics into accessible commentary that reaches both the campus community and the broader public — making health literacy a shared value.

04

Student Recognition

Celebrate student achievement by giving published authors a platform to showcase their work — building portfolios and confidence for future academic and clinical careers.
