Medical Education

From peer teaching to curriculum reform, we empower students to become educators, shape how medicine is taught, and build the skills that matter beyond the classroom.

What we do ↓

01 — What We Do

Building better medical education, together

We run hands-on programmes that turn students into educators, sharpen clinical readiness, and ensure every learner has a voice in how their curriculum evolves.

Peer Teaching

Senior students teaching juniors in small, focused groups. Learning becomes deeper when it's explained by someone who was just in your seat.

Skill Bootcamps

Clinical and soft-skill workshops designed to bridge the gap between textbook knowledge and real-world practice. Intensive, practical, and student-led.

Curriculum Advocacy

Giving students a seat at the table where how medicine is taught gets decided. We channel learner feedback into structural change, not just complaints.

Tutor Academy

Training the next generation of medical educators. We equip students with pedagogy skills, facilitation techniques, and the confidence to lead classrooms.

02 — Our Focus Areas

Where we direct our effort

Four pillars shape every event, project, and initiative that SCOME runs on campus.

01

Peer-Led Learning

Structured peer-teaching sessions that reinforce clinical concepts, build communication skills, and create a culture where students teach students.

02

Clinical Skills Training

Hands-on workshops in history-taking, examination techniques, and procedural skills — closing the gap between theory and bedside practice.

03

Curriculum Reform

Amplifying the student voice in how the medical curriculum is designed and delivered. We advocate for evidence-based, learner-centred teaching.

04

Educational Innovation

Exploring new tools, technologies, and methodologies that make medical education more interactive, inclusive, and effective for every learner.
