How to get involved

Four steps to membership

No prior experience needed. Just bring your curiosity.

01
First

Attend an Intro Session

We run open chapter events throughout the academic year — no registration needed. Come along, meet the team, ask every question you have, and get a feel for what IFMSA KMC is actually like from the inside.

  • Open to all KMC students, any year
  • Sessions announced on our official channels
  • Bring a friend — the more the better
02
Second

Choose Your Committee

IFMSA KMC runs six standing committees, each with a distinct mission. Browse them and identify the one that matches your interests — from research and exchanges to public health and human rights.

SCOPE SCOME SCORA SCOPH SCORP SCORE
Explore all committees →
03
Third

Connect with Your Officers

Each committee is led by Local Committee Officers (LCOs) — fellow KMC students who are here to guide you through everything. Reach out directly, introduce yourself, and they'll walk you through the next steps.

  • No experience required whatsoever
  • LCOs are available throughout the year
  • Ask about current projects and opportunities
04
Final

Sign Up & Get Started

Once you've connected with your LCOs, complete the short membership form and attend your first committee session. You'll be officially part of IFMSA KMC and the worldwide IFMSA network from day one.
