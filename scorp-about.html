What we do ↓

01 — What We Do

On the ground, for those who need it most

SCORP runs year-round initiatives that bring healthcare, relief, and awareness to displaced and underserved communities.

Refugee Outreach

We conduct health checks at displacement centres, providing essential medical screenings and connecting refugees to care they otherwise cannot access.

Peace Week

Dialogue, art, and reflection on medical neutrality — a week-long programme that explores how healthcare workers must remain protected in conflict.

Humanity Trucks

Winter relief collection drives that gather clothing, blankets, and essentials — then deliver them directly to communities battling harsh conditions.

Film Nights

Human rights documentary screenings followed by guided discussion — using film to build empathy and understanding around global health inequity.

02 — Our Focus Areas

The pillars that guide our work

Each initiative maps to one or more of SCORP's core focus areas — the lenses through which we direct action and advocacy.

01

Refugee & Migrant Health

Displaced populations face unique health risks — from infectious disease outbreaks to lack of basic care. SCORP ensures healthcare reaches those without a home to return to.

02

Peace & Medical Neutrality

Hospitals and clinics must be protected under international humanitarian law. SCORP advocates for the inviolability of healthcare in conflict zones.

03

Universal Health Coverage

Health is a human right, not a privilege. SCORP pushes for equitable access to healthcare for every person, regardless of status, income, or location.

04

Humanitarian Relief

When disaster strikes, SCORP mobilises quickly — collecting, coordinating, and delivering supplies to communities in acute need.
