Clinical exchange at its finest. SCOPE sends our students on the world's bedside rotations — from surgical theatre to rural clinics — igniting empathy, hands-on skill and global perspective.

Breaking taboos, one conversation at a time. SCORA drives awareness on SRH and HIV/AIDS rights, consent education, and youth-friendly services across campus and community.

Peer TeachingSoft SkillsCurricular Voice
