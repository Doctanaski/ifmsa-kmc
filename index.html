Know more about us

One student body, every medical student's opportunity.

The Khyber Medical College Local Chapter is part of IFMSA-Pakistan, the sole national member organisation of the International Federation of Medical Students' Associations in Pakistan. Through six standing committees we act on medical education, professional and research exchanges, public health, human rights and peace, and sexual & reproductive health — right here in Peshawar.

  • SCOPE — professional clinical exchanges
  • SCOME — medical education
  • SCORA — sexual & reproductive health
  • SCOPH — public health
  • SCORP — human rights & peace
  • SCORE — research exchanges
