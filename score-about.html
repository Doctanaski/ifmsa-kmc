Research Exchange.

Connecting medical students with research placements abroad, building methodology from the ground up, and mentoring the next generation of medical researchers across borders.

What we do ↓

01 — What We Do

Fueling the research journey

From the first spark of curiosity to published work, SCORE gives students every stepping stone they need to thrive in research.

Summer Research Exchange

Secure bench and laboratory placements abroad during the summer — work alongside active research teams in leading institutions worldwide.

Methodology Bootcamp

From crafting a research question to designing a study — intensive workshops that give you the methodological backbone to plan rigorous research.

Abstract & Publishing Clinic

Turn your research results into polished abstracts and manuscripts. Peer review circles, editing workshops, and journal submission guidance — start to finish.

Research Mentorship

Pair with experienced senior researchers and alumni who've walked the path before you — one-on-one guidance through every phase of your work.

02 — Our Focus Areas

Four pillars, one mission

Every programme we run maps back to one of these four focus areas — the building blocks of a research-ready generation.

01

Research Exchanges

International placements that immerse students in real lab and clinical environments — building cross-border collaboration skills that last a career.

02

Methodology & Rigour

Workshops and bootcamps that teach study design, biostatistics, and critical appraisal — the foundation every strong paper is built on.

03

Publication & Publishing

From abstract writing to journal selection and submission — hands-on clinics that turn finished research into published work.

04

Mentorship & Development

Structured one-on-one mentorship and skills workshops that develop leadership, communication, and the professional confidence to navigate the research world.
