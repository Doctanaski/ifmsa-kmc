What we do ↓

01 — What We Do

Our initiatives on campus

Through campaigns, training sessions, and peer support, we bring sexual and reproductive health education out of the shadows and into everyday student life.

SRH Awareness Campaigns

Breaking taboos on campus through evidence-based awareness drives. We tackle myths, replace stigma with facts, and create safe spaces for honest conversation.

Consent Culture Training

Interactive sessions on boundaries, autonomy, and enthusiastic consent. We equip students with the language and confidence to respect and communicate limits.

Health Awareness Events

Marking key dates like World AIDS Day, Breast Cancer Awareness Month, and more — turning health milestones into campus-wide engagement and education.

Peer Support Line

A confidential, student-run helpline where peers can ask questions, seek guidance, and access support without fear of judgement — always free, always private.

02 — Our Focus Areas

Where we channel our energy

Four pillars guide every campaign, workshop, and initiative SCORA runs at KMC.

01

Destigmatisation

Challenging cultural taboos and misinformation around sexual and reproductive health so that students feel comfortable seeking knowledge and help.

02

Health Education

Delivering accurate, science-backed information on SRH topics — from anatomy and physiology to contraception, STIs, and mental wellness.

03

Consent & Rights

Promoting understanding of bodily autonomy, informed consent, and reproductive rights as fundamental aspects of healthcare and human dignity.

04

Peer Support

Building a network of trained student volunteers who provide confidential guidance, ensuring no student faces SRH questions alone.
