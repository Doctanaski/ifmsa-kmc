Restoring Health, Restoring Dignity

Every meaningful mission has a quiet heartbeat beneath its purpose, and for Project Hope, that heartbeat was human need. From April to September, our efforts became a window of possibility for those connected to KMC students and staff.

01 — Our Story

A window of possibility

Each one was a person behind a case, a life waiting to be seen. We didn't just process cases; we entered the space between fear and relief, suffering and the first breath of care.

Each case was not a limitation but a promise of full attention and care, quietly and responsibly given where it was needed most. What started as purpose and process grew into something greater: a door opening where there had only been uncertainty, and a light returning to eyes that had stopped expecting it.

Each closed case was not an end but a moment of exhale, a sign of hope. Project Hope proves that even within limits of time and resources, compassion can lead the way, restoring health and the dignity of being cared for.

02 — Our Cases

Lives we touched

FromApril to September 2026, every case was a story of trust, compassion, and a community stepping forward.

April 2026

A Child's Monthly Lifeline

An 8-year-old child, the younger brother of a KMC student, had thalassemia major. He required monthly blood transfusions and daily medication. His medications cost approximately PKR 11,000 per month. The family did not have the financial means to consistently manage his medical care, including regular blood transfusions and medications.

Target: PKR 65,000 Case Closed
May 2026

Hearing the World Again

A KMC student and his younger brother had hearing difficulties and had been using low-quality hearing devices since childhood due to financial constraints. Over time, it had become increasingly difficult for the student to function in busy and crowded environments, and his hearing had further deteriorated. As he was required to work in the hospital and attend clinical wards, having a proper hearing device was essential for effective communication and participation in his clinical training.

Target: PKR 160,000 Case Closed
June 2026

Thirty-Eight Days of Hope

A final-year student from KMC had reached out for support regarding his sister, who had been admitted to the ICU at HMC for 38 days. Although the Sehat Card covered most of her treatment, several essential medications remained uncovered. The family was in urgent need of financial assistance, and contributions were needed to help continue her care.

Target: PKR 50,000 Case Closed
Project Amaan

Welfare Drive for Orphanages

Project Amaan conducted a welfare drive for three orphanages — Khyber Kor, Dar-ul-Atfaal, and Fixit — to support children in need and bring them moments of happiness, care, and hope. The initiative aimed to raise Rs. 100,000, which was used to provide Eid gifts, organize fun activities and interactive sessions, and conduct public health awareness campaigns. The drive also included career counselling and mentorship sessions, pubertal health education for young girls, and sanitary pad distribution along with menstrual hygiene awareness. The initiative focused not only on providing material support but also on promoting dignity, education, confidence, and opportunities for a better future.

Target: PKR 100,000 Case Closed
